# RIMKEEP whitepaper

## The job
Next lander ellipses overlap Apollo hardware, Surveyor parts, and other CLPS wreckage. Lighting tools never see that. RIMKEEP takes a catalog of known surface assets, a keep-out radius (heritage + plume), and a planned ellipse. It reports overlap area and a simple conjunction flag.

v1 is geometry. Not legal advice. Not ITU. Not orbital SSA.
